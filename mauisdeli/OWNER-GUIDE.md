# How to Update Your Website

Welcome! This is a quick guide for updating your Maui's Deli website yourself.

## Logging in

1. Go to: **https://mauisdeli.com/admin/**
2. Click **Login with GitHub**.
3. You're in.

You can do this from your phone, tablet, or computer. It's mobile-friendly.

---

## What you can change

On the left side of the admin, you'll see seven sections:

### 🍔 Menu Items
Add new dishes, change prices, write descriptions, upload photos, or hide items that are sold out.

**To update a price:** Click Menu Items → click the item → change the price → **Publish**.
**To hide an item temporarily:** Turn off "Available today?" and publish. It'll disappear from the public menu until you switch it back on.

### 🥪 Sub of the Week
One sandwich you want to spotlight this week. Shows on the homepage and specials page.

**To change it:** Click Sub of the Week → update the name, description, price, or photo → **Publish**.

### 💥 Weekly Specials
Your $9.99 daily specials (or any other promos).

**To change Monday's special:** Click Weekly Specials → find the Monday row → edit → **Publish**.
**To add a new special:** Click **+ Add Special** at the bottom of the list.

### 📢 Community Note
The bar that shows at the top of every page. Use it for short updates:
- "Closing early Friday for the holiday"
- "Thanks for the local support!"
- "Catering now available"

**To turn it off:** Switch off "Show this announcement?" and publish.

### ⭐ Featured Dishes
The "House Favorites" section on the homepage.

**To swap in a new favorite:** Click Featured Dishes → edit or add an item → upload a photo → **Publish**.
**To change the order they appear:** Lower "Sort order" numbers show first (1, 2, 3…).

### 📸 Gallery Photos
Real food photos on the Gallery page.

**To add a new photo:** Click Gallery Photos → **+ Add Photo** → upload → give it a title → **Publish**.
**To reorder:** Adjust the "Sort order" numbers.

### 🕒 Hours
Your open hours for each day of the week.

**To change a day's hours:** Click Hours → edit that day's "Hours" field (e.g. "7:00 AM – 7:00 PM" or "Closed") → **Publish**.
**To add a holiday note:** Use the "Note" field at the bottom.

---

## Saving changes

After any edit, click the blue **Publish** button in the top-right.
Your changes will be live on the website in about 30 seconds.

---

## Tips

- **Photos:** Square or landscape photos look best. They'll be automatically resized.
- **Phone typing:** The admin works great on phone. Save as you go.
- **Something broke?** Every change is saved in version history — nothing's ever lost. Call whoever set this up for you if you need help rolling back.

---

## What you cannot change from the admin
(and why)

Things like page layout, colors, phone number, and address are set in the website code to keep everything consistent. If one of those needs to change, just text your web person — it's a 2-minute fix.
