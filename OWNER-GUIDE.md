# How to Update Your Website

Welcome! This is a quick guide for updating your Maui's Deli & Shop website yourself, through your private admin area — **Maui's Office**.

## Logging in

1. Go to: **https://mauisdeli.com/admin/**
2. Click **Log In** — that opens the Netlify Identity sign-in box.
3. Enter the email and password from your invite. (First time? Use the link in your invite email to set your password.)
4. You're in.

You can do this from your phone, tablet, or computer. It's mobile-friendly.

---

## What you can change

On the left side of Maui's Office, you'll see eight sections:

### 🍽️ Menu Items
Add new dishes, change prices, write descriptions, upload photos, or hide items that are sold out. This is also where Neighborhood Favorites and Weekly Specials are controlled.

**To update a price:** Click Menu Items → click the item → change the price → **Publish**.
**To hide an item temporarily:** Set Status to **Hidden** and Publish. It'll disappear from the public site until you switch it back to Active.
**To feature an item on the homepage:** Toggle **Show in Neighborhood Favorites** on, and Publish.
**To make an item the daily special:** Pick the days under **Weekly Special days**, and Publish.

### 🥪 Sub of the Week
One sandwich you want to spotlight this week. Shows on the homepage and specials page.

**To change it:** Click Sub of the Week → use **Pick a Menu Item** to select an item by name → **Publish**. (The site will use that menu item's name, photo, description, and price automatically.)

### 💸 Weekly Specials (legacy)
A backup list for days when the special is something **not** on the regular menu. Most of the time, just leave this alone — Menu Items handles weekly specials now.

### 📋 Community Board
Neighborhood posts, shop updates, local events, fundraisers. Up to 3 show on the homepage; the rest live on the specials page.

**To add a post:** Click Community Board → **+ Add Post** → fill in title, description, category → **Publish**.
**To pin a post to the top:** Toggle **Pinned**.
**To hide a post without deleting it:** Toggle **Active** off.

### 📣 Store News / Announcement
The bar that shows at the top of every page. Use it for short updates:
- "Closing early Friday for the holiday"
- "Thanks for the local support!"
- "Catering now available"

**To turn it off:** Switch off "Show this announcement?" and publish.

### 📷 Gallery Photos
Real food photos on the Gallery page.

**To add a new photo:** Click Gallery Photos → **+ Add Photo** → upload → give it a title → **Publish**.
**To reorder:** Adjust the "Sort order" numbers.

### 🕐 Hours
Your open hours for each day of the week.

**To change a day's hours:** Click Hours → edit that day's "Hours" field (e.g. "7:00 AM – 7:00 PM" or "Closed") → **Publish**.
**To add a holiday note:** Use the "Note" field at the bottom.

### ⚙️ Settings
Turn whole homepage sections on or off, and flip "coming soon" labels for breakfast / ice cream / beer & wine.

---

## Saving changes

After any edit, click the blue **Publish** button in the top-right.
Your changes will be live on the website in about 30 seconds.

---

## Tips

- **Photos:** Square or landscape photos look best. They'll be automatically resized.
- **Phone typing:** Maui's Office works great on phone. Save as you go.
- **Something broke?** Every change is saved in version history (in the GitHub repo) — nothing's ever lost. Call whoever set this up for you if you need help rolling back.

---

## What you cannot change from Maui's Office
(and why)

Things like page layout, colors, phone number, and address are set in the website code to keep everything consistent. If one of those needs to change, just text your web person — it's a 2-minute fix.
