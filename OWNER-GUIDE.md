# How to Update Your Website

Welcome! This is a quick guide for updating your Maui's Deli & Shop website yourself.

The admin area is called **Maui's Office**. That's your private space to update the site. The public website still says "Maui's Deli & Shop" — only the editor area is called "Maui's Office."

## Logging in to Maui's Office

1. Go to: **https://mauisdeli.com/admin/**
2. Enter the email and password you set up when you got your invite.
3. You're in.

You can do this from your phone, tablet, or computer. It's mobile-friendly.

If you forgot your password, there's a "Forgot password?" link on the login screen — it'll email you a reset link.

---

## What you can change

On the left side of Maui's Office, you'll see eight sections:

### 🍽️ Menu Items
This is the main one. Add new dishes, change prices, write descriptions, upload photos, hide items that are sold out, mark things as Coming Soon, control which items appear on the homepage, and assign daily specials.

**To update a price:** Click Menu Items → click the item → change the price → **Publish**.

**To hide an item:** Open the item, set **Status** to **Hidden**, and publish. It disappears from the menu, the homepage, the specials, and Sub of the Week — everywhere — until you switch it back to Active.

**To mark something Coming Soon:** Open the item, set **Status** to **Coming Soon**. The item shows on the menu with an orange "Coming Soon" badge and no price.

**To feature an item on the homepage:** Toggle **Show in Neighborhood Favorites** ON. The item appears in the homepage Neighborhood Favorites section automatically.

**To make an item the daily special:** Open the item, in **Weekly Special days** pick the day(s) — Monday, Tuesday, etc. The item shows on the homepage Specials and the Specials page on those days at the $9.99 price.

### 🥪 Sub of the Week
One sandwich (or any menu item, really) to spotlight on the homepage and specials page.

**To change it:** Click Sub of the Week → use the **Pick a Menu Item** dropdown to choose which item to feature → **Publish**. The site automatically pulls the name, description, price, and photo from the menu item — so editing the menu item updates the spotlight too.

### 💸 Weekly Specials (legacy)
This is mostly automatic now — most weekday specials are controlled in Menu Items via the "Weekly Special days" picker. This section is a fallback for days when the special is something not on the regular menu (e.g. a one-off Cheese Burger Wednesday).

### 📋 Community Board
Posts that show on the homepage and the What's New page. Use this for shop updates, local events, fundraisers, school announcements, anything community-flavored.

**To add a post:** Click Community Board → **+ Add** → fill in title, description, optional photo → **Publish**.

**To pin something:** Toggle **Pinned** ON — pinned posts always show first.

### 📣 Store News / Announcement
The thin banner at the top of every page. Use it for short updates:
- "Closing early Friday for the holiday"
- "Thanks for the local support!"
- "Catering now available"

**To turn it off:** Switch off "Show this announcement?" and publish.

### 📷 Gallery Photos
Real food photos on the Gallery page.

**To add a new photo:** Click Gallery Photos → **+ Add Photo** → upload → give it a title → **Publish**.
**To reorder:** Adjust the "Sort order" numbers.

### 🕐 Hours
Your open hours for each day of the week.

**To change a day's hours:** Click Hours → edit that day's "Hours" field (e.g. "7:00 AM – 7:00 PM" or "Closed") → **Publish**.
**To add a holiday note:** Use the "Note" field at the bottom.

### ⚙️ Settings
Turn whole homepage sections on or off, and flip "Coming Soon" labels for things like Breakfast, Ice Cream, and Beer & Wine.

---

## Saving changes

After any edit, click the blue **Publish** button in the top-right.
Your changes will be live on the website in about 30 seconds.

---

## Tips

- **Photos:** Square or landscape photos look best. They'll be automatically resized.
- **Phone typing:** The admin works great on phone. Save as you go.
- **Something broke?** Every change is saved in version history — nothing's ever lost. Call whoever set this up for you if you need help rolling back.

---

## What you cannot change from the admin
(and why)

Things like page layout, colors, phone number, and address are set in the website code to keep everything consistent. If one of those needs to change, just text your web person — it's a 2-minute fix.
